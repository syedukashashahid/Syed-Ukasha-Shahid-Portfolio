Web security scanner

CyberShield

Status
In development
Built with
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Flask, SQLite

A web security scanner for websites its user is authorized to test. It is designed to identify security weaknesses and generate reports that give each finding a severity level and a recommended fix.

What it is designed to do

  • Works on authorized websites
  • Identifies security weaknesses
  • Generates reports with severity levels
  • Recommends fixes for findings

The screenshot

Scroll sideways to see the whole screen.

ScreenshotScreenshot of the CyberShield dashboard from the current development build, signed in with a demo account.

Visible in this screenshot

  • An overall risk score out of 100, with a letter grade, for the latest scan
  • Totals for scans performed, findings recorded and open critical or high findings
  • A list of recent scans, each with its mix of severities and a risk score
  • Open findings split into critical, high, medium, low and informational
  • Navigation for new scans, verified sites, scan history and scan comparison
